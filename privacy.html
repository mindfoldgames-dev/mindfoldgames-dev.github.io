Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026  |  Last Updated: September 27, 2026

MindFold Games ("we", "us", or "our") operates Arrow Escape: Logic Puzzle (the "App"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We do NOT collect personally identifiable information.

Arrow Escape does not require an account, login, or registration. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal data.

Automatically Collected Information

The App may automatically collect certain non-personal technical information for analytics and crash reporting purposes:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Usage Data: App launch events, levels played, in-app purchases, and session duration.
  • Crash Reports: Diagnostic information when the App crashes, including stack traces and device state.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — To serve advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing — To process in-app purchases. Transaction data is handled by Google. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — To understand how players use the App (aggregate, non-personal analytics). Firebase Privacy

3. Advertising

Arrow Escape displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use cookies and device identifiers to serve personalized ads based on your interests. You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

4. In-App Purchases

Arrow Escape offers optional in-app purchases (coins, power-ups, ad removal) processed through Google Play Billing. We do not store or have access to your payment information — all transactions are handled by Google Play.

5. Children's Privacy

Arrow Escape is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

6. Data Retention

All game progress (levels, coins, achievements) is stored locally on your device using Android's DataStore. We do not store your game data on our servers. Uninstalling the App will delete all local data.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Request information about what data is collected

Since we do not collect personal data, most of these rights are automatically satisfied.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
